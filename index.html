Timeline

1892
The Founding

Liverpool FC formed at Anfield after a dispute between Everton and the ground's landlord, beginning a journey that would transform a city's identity.

1965
Shankly's First FA Cup

Bill Shankly's Liverpool won the FA Cup for the first time, cementing a cultural revolution at the club and laying the groundwork for decades of dominance.

1977
First European Cup

A 3–1 victory over Borussia Mönchengladbach in Rome announced Liverpool as continental giants. The first of four European Cups in eight years.

1984
Fourth European Cup

Liverpool defeated AS Roma on penalties in their own stadium to claim a fourth European Cup — the peak of Bob Paisley and Joe Fagan's legendary era.

2005
The Miracle of Istanbul

Three goals down at half-time against AC Milan, Liverpool completed one of sport's most extraordinary comebacks to win the Champions League on penalties.

2019
Champions League — Madrid

Under Jürgen Klopp, Liverpool defeated Tottenham 2–0 in Madrid to claim a sixth European Cup and end a decade-long wait for silverware.

2020
Premier League Champions

After 30 years, Klopp's side clinched the title with seven games to spare — the fastest team to be crowned champions in Premier League history.

2025
Arne Slot Era Begins

New manager Arne Slot leads Liverpool to the Premier League title in his debut season, continuing the club's run as one of Europe's elite.
